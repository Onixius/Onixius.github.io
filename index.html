Source Productions Publisher

Source Productions Publisher is the internal publishing tool of Source Productions, a video production studio.

It uploads videos made by Source Productions to our own TikTok account as drafts, so we can review and publish them from the TikTok app, and it reads back basic statistics for those posts (views, likes, comments, shares).

The tool is used only by Source Productions for its own accounts. It is not offered to the public.
