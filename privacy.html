Privacy Policy

Source Productions Publisher · Effective date: November 9, 2026

1. Scope

This policy covers Source Productions Publisher (“the App”), an internal tool that Source Productions uses only for its own TikTok account. The App has no public users.

2. Data the App accesses

With the account owner’s permission, given on TikTok’s authorization screen, the App accesses:

  • basic profile information of the connected TikTok account (account ID, display name, avatar);
  • permission to upload videos to that account as drafts;
  • public statistics of the account’s own videos (views, likes, comments count, shares).

The App does not access private messages, contacts, or data of any other TikTok users.

3. How the data is used

Only to upload Source Productions’ own videos and to show Source Productions how those videos perform.

4. Storage and security

Access tokens are stored only on Source Productions’ own computer and are never published. Statistics are kept locally for internal reporting.

5. Sharing

Data is not sold, rented or shared with third parties.

6. Revoking access and deletion

The account owner can revoke the App’s access at any time in the TikTok app settings. After that, locally stored tokens and statistics are deleted.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated. The effective date above shows the latest version.
